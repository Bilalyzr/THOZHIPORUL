## Production Notes

### **Visual Style Guide**
- **Color Palette**: Blue (#1F4E79), Green (#2E7D32), Orange (#E67E22)
- **Transitions**: Smooth fade-ins, slide animations, zoom effects
- **Screen Recording**: 1080p or higher, smooth mouse movements
- **Text Overlays**: Clean, sans-serif fonts, adequate contrast

### **Music & Sound Effects**
- Background: Corporate, uplifting, instrumental
- Transitions: Subtle swoosh sounds
- Success moments: Pleasant notification sounds
- Avoid: Distinctive sound effects that might distract

### **Screen Recording Tips**
1. Use a clean browser (no bookmarks/extensions visible)
2. Set browser to 100% zoom
3. Hide mouse cursor in edits where not needed
4. Maintain consistent speed - not too fast, not too slow
5. Use mock data that looks realistic

### **Key Features to Highlight**
- ✅ Role-based access (Admin, Industry, Government)
- ✅ Real-time compliance tracking
- ✅ GIS-based Parks Explorer
- ✅ One-click plot allotment
- ✅ Transparent service request tracking
- ✅ Digital, paperless operations
