## Voice Recording Tips

### **Before Recording**
1. ✅ Find a quiet room with minimal echo
2. ✅ Use a quality microphone (USB condenser mic recommended)
3. ✅ Warm up your voice - read the script aloud once
4. ✅ Drink water to keep your throat hydrated

### **During Recording**
1. ✅ Speak clearly and at a moderate pace
2. ✅ Maintain consistent distance from microphone (6-8 inches)
3. ✅ Emphasize key words: "THOZHIRPORUL", "Real-time", "One-click", "100% digital"
4. ✅ Take natural breaths between sentences
5. ✅ Smile while speaking - it makes your voice sound more friendly

### **Technical Settings**
- Sample Rate: 44.1kHz or 48kHz
- Bit Depth: 16-bit or 24-bit
- Format: WAV (uncompressed) or high-quality MP3 (320kbps)
- Remove background noise in post-production
- Add compression for consistent volume levels
