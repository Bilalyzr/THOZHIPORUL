# THOZHIRPORUL - Complete Software Walkthrough Script
**Duration:** 3-5 minutes  
**Comprehensive coverage of all features for Admin and Industry roles**

---

## INTRODUCTION (0:00 - 0:15)

"Welcome to THOZHIRPORUL - Tamil Nadu's Smart Industrial Monitoring System. This comprehensive platform, built by NEXORA in partnership with the Government of Tamil Nadu, is transforming how industries, administrators, and government officers interact and collaborate. Today, we'll take you through a complete walkthrough of the platform from both the Administrator and Industry user perspectives."

---

## PART 1: ROLE SELECTION & LOGIN (0:15 - 0:40)

"Getting started is simple. Upon visiting the platform, you're presented with three distinct portals - each designed for a specific user role.

The **Administrator Portal** is for SIPCOT officials who need full system control.

The **Industry Portal** is for manufacturing units and industrial companies to manage their compliance, submit data, and request services.

And the **Government Officer Portal** is for district and state-level oversight.

Let's begin with the **Industry Portal** to see how industries experience the platform."

---

## PART 2: INDUSTRY DASHBOARD (0:40 - 1:30)

### **Industry Workspace Overview (0:40 - 0:55)**

"Once logged in, industry users are greeted with their personalized Workspace Dashboard. The first thing you notice is your **Compliance Score** - displayed prominently at the top. This score, ranging from 0 to 100, reflects your overall compliance status across environmental, safety, financial, and operational categories.

A green badge means you're compliant - eligible for all benefits and auto-approvals. Yellow indicates you need attention, and red means there are issues requiring immediate action."

### **Quick Actions & Stats (0:55 - 1:10)**

"Below your compliance score, you'll find quick-action buttons and key statistics. View your total employees, current investment, and recent activity at a glance. The dashboard is designed to give you a complete snapshot of your industrial operation in one view.

The sidebar navigation gives you access to all major features - which we'll explore in detail."

### **Data Submission (1:10 - 1:30)**

"Let's start with **Data Submission**. THOZHIRPORUL has completely digitized the quarterly reporting process. No more paperwork. Just fill in your financial data - revenue, capital expenditure, R&D spending. Add your employment numbers with breakdowns for direct and indirect jobs. Log your resource consumption - electricity, water, and waste generation.

And for those making a social impact, there's a section for CSR activities. Once filled, just click submit, and your compliance score automatically updates based on your submission."

---

## PART 3: SERVICE REQUESTS (1:30 - 2:15)

### **Services Overview (1:30 - 1:45)**

"Next, let's explore the **Services Tracker** - your gateway to all government approvals and services. Whether you need a Fire NOC, Pollution Control Certificate, Building Plan Approval, or even a new water or power connection - everything is handled here.

The Kanban board view shows all your requests organized by status - from Applied, through Document Review and Field Inspection, all the way to Approval and Completion. Complete transparency at every stage."

### **Requesting a New Service (1:45 - 2:00)**

"When you need a new service, simply click 'New Request'. Select the service type, add priority if urgent, attach any required documents, and submit. You'll receive a unique reference number for tracking.

For example, requesting a Fire Safety NOC typically takes 7 days. A Pollution Control NOC takes 10 days. And you can track progress in real-time, with estimated completion dates and assigned officer details."

---

## PART 4: PARKS EXPLORER & PLOT APPLICATION (2:15 - 3:00)

### **Interactive GIS Map (2:15 - 2:30)**

"One of the most powerful features is the **Parks Explorer**. This interactive GIS map displays all six major SIPCOT industrial parks across Tamil Nadu - Oragadam, Sriperumbudur, Hosur, Cheyyar, Thoothukudi, and Gangaikondan.

Click on any park marker to see detailed information - total area, available plots, number of industries, infrastructure score, and more. Switch between street, satellite, and terrain views to get the perspective you need."

### **Applying for Industrial Plot (2:30 - 2:50)**

"When you're ready to expand or set up a new unit, applying for land is seamless. Select your desired park, check the available area, and click 'Apply for Plot'. The system intelligently navigates you to the service request form with your park already pre-selected.

Just enter your required area in acres, choose your priority level, add any remarks, and submit. Your application is immediately visible to SIPCOT administrators for processing."

### **Viewing Available Plots (2:50 - 3:00)**

"Once logged in, you can even view detailed plot information within each park - including plot dimensions, location within the park, and current availability status. This level of transparency helps you make informed decisions about your investment."

---

## PART 5: COMPLIANCE MONITORING (3:00 - 3:30)

"Your **Compliance Monitoring** section gives you detailed insights into your compliance status. View your scores broken down by category - Environmental, Safety, Financial, and Operational. See submission history and track upcoming deadlines.

If there are any violations or issues, you'll see them here with clear descriptions and suggested corrective actions. This proactive approach helps you maintain good standing and avoid penalties."

---

## PART 6: REPORTS & ANALYTICS (3:30 - 3:50)

"Need official documents? Head to the **Report Center**. Generate compliance reports, utilization summaries, investment statements, and more. Export them as PDF or Excel for your records.

The **Analytics Dashboard** provides visual insights into your data over time - track your growth, resource consumption patterns, and compliance trends."

---

## PART 7: SWITCHING TO ADMIN PORTAL (3:50 - 4:00)

"Now, let's switch to the **Administrator Portal** to see how SIPCOT officials manage the entire ecosystem. After logging out, administrators select the Admin Portal and enter their credentials to access the full system control panel."

---

## PART 8: ADMIN DASHBOARD (4:00 - 4:40)

### **Global KPIs & Real-time Monitoring (4:00 - 4:20)**

"The **Admin Dashboard** is the command center for the entire system. At the top, administrators see global Key Performance Indicators - total industries across all parks, combined investment in crores, total direct and indirect employment generated, and real-time utility monitoring showing current electricity and water consumption across all parks.

The growth charts show industry establishment trends over 5 years, while resource consumption graphs help with infrastructure planning."

### **AI Decision Support (4:20 - 4:40)**

"What makes THOZHIRPORUL truly intelligent is the **AI Decision Support** system. It automatically analyzes all industry data and flags critical items that need attention. For example, if an industry's water usage spikes beyond permitted limits, or if compliance drops suddenly, the AI brings it to the administrator's attention.

The system even suggests actions - like scheduling inspections or issuing notices - and can execute routine approvals automatically for high-compliance industries."

---

## PART 9: SERVICES TRACKER - ADMIN VIEW (4:40 - 5:20)

### **Managing All Requests (4:40 - 5:00)**

"In the **Services Tracker**, administrators see ALL service requests from all industries in one place. The Kanban board makes it easy to identify requests by status - Applied, Under Review, Pending Approval, or Completed.

Administrators can filter by service type, priority, or park. The bottleneck analysis feature helps identify which services are taking longer and which officers might need support."

### **Plot Allotment Feature (5:00 - 5:20)**

"When an industry applies for land allotment and completes all necessary reviews, administrators can directly allot a specific plot. Click on the request, then click the green 'Allot Plot' button.

The system shows all available plots in the requested park with complete details - plot number, area in acres, dimensions, rate per acre, and location within the park. Simply select the appropriate plot, add any remarks, and confirm.

The system automatically updates three things at once: marks the plot as allotted, assigns it to the industry's profile, and completes the service request. This atomic operation ensures data consistency across the system."

---

## PART 10: COMPLIANCE ENGINE (5:20 - 6:00)

"The **Compliance Engine** gives administrators complete visibility into compliance across all industries. View the overall compliance rate, drill down by park or category, and see all violations with severity ratings.

The system automatically detects violations based on predefined rules - missed deadlines, usage thresholds, expired certifications - and categorizes them by severity: low, medium, high, or critical. Administrators can assign actions, track resolutions, and generate compliance reports."

---

## PART 11: USER MANAGEMENT (6:00 - 6:20)

"The **User Management** module allows administrators to oversee all registered users - both industries and government officers. View account status, activate or deactivate accounts, and manage user access. This ensures that only authorized personnel have access to sensitive data and functions."

---

## PART 12: ANALYTICS & REPORTING (6:20 - 6:45)

"Administrators have access to powerful **Analytics Dashboards** showing state-wide trends, district-wise comparisons, and park performance rankings. Track investment flows, employment generation, and infrastructure utilization.

The **Report Center** can generate any type of report - compliance summaries, utilization reports, service delivery metrics - and export them for government presentations and policy planning."

---

## PART 13: SETTINGS & CONFIGURATION (6:45 - 7:00)

"Finally, the **Settings** panel allows administrators to configure system-wide parameters, manage workflow automation rules, and control notification preferences. The system is highly customizable to adapt to changing policies and requirements."

---

## CONCLUSION (7:00 - 7:20)

"THOZHIRPORUL represents a complete digital transformation of industrial governance in Tamil Nadu. From the industry's perspective - seamless compliance management, easy service requests, and complete transparency. From the administrator's perspective - centralized control, AI-powered insights, and efficient workflow management.

The result: 1,250+ industries monitored, 95% compliance rate achieved, service delivery reduced from 30 days to just 7 days, and 100% paperless operations.

THOZHIRPORUL - Smart Industrial Monitoring System. The future of industrial governance is here. Visit thozhirporul.tn.gov.in for more information."
